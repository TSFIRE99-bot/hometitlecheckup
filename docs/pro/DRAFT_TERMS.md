# Draft pilot terms and privacy notice

**DRAFT. Not reviewed by an attorney. Do not send, sign or publish.** These are outlines for an Ohio attorney to
review and rewrite. Some founders are under 18, which affects who can sign contracts for HomeKeep; an adult (or an
entity formed by adults) may need to be the contracting party. Ask the reviewing attorney.

## Pilot agreement outline

1. **Parties.** The firm, and [the HomeKeep contracting party, to be decided with an attorney].
2. **What's provided.** Access to HomeKeep Pro for 30 days for up to [10] new matters, up to [4] hours of support.
3. **Price.** $[500], invoiced once, payable by [method]. No automatic renewal. No overage charges.
4. **What HomeKeep is not.** Not legal advice, not a title opinion, not a determination of heirs or shares. The firm's
   attorneys remain responsible for all legal work. Machine-suggested facts may be wrong and must be reviewed.
5. **Firm responsibilities.** Decide what to collect, get any consent the firm's ethics rules require from families,
   review every fact, and send any follow-up themselves.
6. **Data ownership.** The firm owns its case data. HomeKeep uses it only to provide the service to that firm.
7. **No sale or sharing.** HomeKeep doesn't sell, share or use firm or family data for marketing, prospect lists or
   training any model.
8. **Service providers.** List each one that touches data (hosting, database, email if connected), what it receives,
   and where. Today: Vercel (hosting and logs). Add the database and email providers before any real data.
9. **AI providers.** None today. HomeKeep won't send firm documents to any AI provider without the firm's written
   approval naming the provider.
10. **Security.** Describe what is actually in place (see `/pro/security`), and what isn't. No certification claims.
11. **Retention and deletion.** The firm sets retention (30 to 3,650 days after closing). At the end of the pilot the
    firm may export everything, and HomeKeep deletes the firm's data within [30] days of a written request.
12. **Incidents.** HomeKeep tells the firm within [72 hours] of learning of unauthorized access to its data.
13. **Case study.** Only with separate written permission after the pilot.
14. **Ending early.** Either side can end the pilot with written notice; [refund terms to be decided].
15. **Liability, insurance and governing law.** For the attorney to draft. Ohio law.

## Privacy notice outline (HomeKeep Pro)

1. Who runs HomeKeep Pro, and how to reach us.
2. What we collect from firm staff: name, email, password hash, actions in the workspace.
3. What we collect from family members a firm invites: name, email (if the firm adds it), relationship, documents and
   answers they choose to send, and when they agreed to email.
4. Why: only to let the firm handle that family's matter.
5. Who sees it: the inviting firm's staff, and our service providers listed by name.
6. What we never do: sell it, share it with other firms, market to families, or train AI models on it.
7. How long we keep it: the firm's retention setting; deletion on request through the firm.
8. Security measures in place, stated plainly, with what isn't in place yet.
9. Family members' choices: they can stop using the link at any time, ask the firm to delete what they sent, and
   withdraw email consent.
10. Changes to the notice and the date it was last updated.
