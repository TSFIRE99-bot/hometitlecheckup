# HomeKeep Pro pilot (draft for founder review)

**Draft.** Prices, limits and terms are hypotheses. Nothing here is an offer until the founders approve it and an
attorney has reviewed the pilot agreement. Don't send this to a firm before then.

## Who it's for

Small Ohio law offices, starting in Franklin County, that handle probate or estate matters where a house is involved.

## What it does

Gets inherited-home cases ready for attorney review with less document chasing.

- Staff open a case and send the family a private link. Family members upload a deed, death certificate, tax bill or
  will from a phone, and type in who's in the family. No account needed.
- HomeKeep reads text from ordinary PDFs and suggests facts (names, dates, parcel numbers), each tied to the page it
  came from. Staff approve, correct or reject every one. The reading is simple pattern matching: it works on our
  fictional demo files but hasn't been tested on real deeds or certificates. Scans and photos are read with free OCR in the staff member's own browser, so files never go to another company; OCR makes mistakes, so staff check every value.
  Staff can always type a fact in by hand.
- It flags missing items and conflicts, such as a death date that differs between the certificate and the family's
  note.
- Follow-ups use only templates an attorney approved, and only for people who agreed to email. Sending isn't
  connected yet: HomeKeep records the message and staff send it from their own email.
- At the end, staff export a packet: summary, document list, approved facts with sources, open conflicts and missing
  items, marked for professional review.

## What it doesn't do

It doesn't give legal advice, determine heirs or ownership shares, certify title, draft deeds, file anything, or
send messages on its own. It doesn't connect to Clio or other practice software yet. Machine reading makes mistakes,
which is why every fact needs a person's review.

## What we ask in return

- Two short interviews: one before the pilot about how intake works today, one after.
- A rough time note per matter (minutes spent chasing documents), so we can compare with the firm's own baseline.
- Permission to use the results in a case study only if the firm agrees in writing afterward. Saying no changes
  nothing else.

## Who we are

HomeKeep is built by four Central Ohio high school students. We built the free HomeKeep checkup for homeowners
first, and Pro is our attempt to help the law offices those families end up calling. We're not a law firm, we don't
have security certifications, and we'll tell you exactly what we have and haven't built.
