# What is a tangled title?

**"Grandma's house is still in Grandpa's name."**

When someone who owns a home dies, the home doesn't move to the family on its own. Someone has to file paperwork with
the County or go through probate court. If nobody does, the family keeps living there, but legally the home still
belongs to the person who died. That's called a **tangled title** (or *heirs' property*).

## With every generation it gets harder to fix

Every one of those owners has to be found, and has to sign, before the home can be sold, refinanced, or repaired with
a loan. [Try it with your own family](/inherit), and see how much of the home's value gets locked.

## What it costs a family {alert}

- Without a clear title, families can't get home-repair financing, claim the homestead tax break, get disaster aid, or
  borrow against the home (LISC, 2024).
- Selling or refinancing usually needs every heir to agree and sign, and some heirs may be hard to find.
- Unpaid property taxes can still lead to foreclosure while the title is tangled.

## How big is it? {users}

- Ohio has an estimated 9,000 heirs' properties (Greater Ohio Policy Center, 2024).
- Those homes hold about $313 million in family wealth that clear titles could unlock (LISC, 2024).
- Experts think it's concentrated in neighborhoods that faced long-term disinvestment, where families had less access
  to lawyers and estate planning.

## The easy fix most people don't know about {shield}

Ohio has a **Transfer on Death Designation Affidavit**: a short form the owner signs in front of a notary and records
with the County Recorder for $34. When the owner dies, the home goes straight to the people named, with no probate. It
has to be recorded **while the owner is alive**. Free forms:
[Transfer on Death affidavit forms (Franklin County Law Library)](https://fclawlib.libguides.com/TOD/transferondeathaffidavit).

If the owner has already died, the family usually needs probate court. Small estates can use a shorter process.
[Check your home](/) to see which steps fit your family.
