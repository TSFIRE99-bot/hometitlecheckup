How it works

  1. Open a matter.
    Staff add the property and choose what to collect from a checklist your attorney approves.
  2. Invite family.
    Each relative gets an expiring phone-friendly link that shows only what they were asked for.
  3. Read documents.
    Text-based PDFs are read and proposed facts are shown next to the exact line they came from. The reading is simple pattern matching, tested only on fictional files so far; scans and photos aren't read yet.
  4. Review and follow up.
    Staff approve or correct each fact. Conflicts and missing items are listed; follow-ups use templates you approve.
  5. Export the packet.
    A PDF and JSON packet with the document inventory, approved facts, evidence, open questions and missing items.

What it does not do

  • It does not give legal advice, determine heirs, calculate legally binding shares, certify title, draft deeds or file anything.
  • It treats the County Auditor's owner field as a tax record, not proof of title, and never assumes anyone has died.
  • It never sends a message on its own. A staff member reviews and sends every follow-up.
  • It does not sell or share homeowner information. The free homeowner checkup at myhomekeep.org stays free and never feeds this workspace.
