How a case moves

Five steps, all done by your staff. HomeKeep keeps track of what's done and what isn't.

  1. Open the matterAdd the property and your matter number. The checklist of what to collect is one your attorney approves once for the firm.
  2. Send family a linkEach relative gets their own link that works on a phone, expires in 14 days, and shows only what they were asked for. No account.
  3. Read the documentsHomeKeep reads the text in ordinary PDFs and suggests facts, each next to the line it came from. It's simple pattern matching, tested only on our fictional files so far, and it can't read scans or photos yet. Staff can always type a fact in.
  4. Review and follow upApprove, correct or reject each fact. Conflicts and missing items stay listed until someone deals with them. Follow-ups use only templates your attorney approved.
  5. Export the packetA PDF and a data file with the document list, approved facts and where each came from, open conflicts, and what's still missing, marked for professional review.

What it won't do

  • Give legal advice, decide who the heirs are, work out legal shares, certify title, draft deeds or file anything.
  • Treat the County Auditor's owner name as proof of title, or assume anyone has died.
  • Send a message on its own. A person at your office sends every follow-up.
  • Sell or share anyone's information. The free homeowner checkup stays free and never feeds this workspace.

Where the documents go

Files are checked when they arrive, kept apart from every other firm, and can be downloaded only by your signed-in staff. Anything a family sends waits in quarantine until someone at your office releases it.

Some things aren't in place yet, like malware scanning and a permanent database. The data handling page lists them plainly. Read how documents are handled.
