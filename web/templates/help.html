Free help near you

You don't have to do this alone, and you shouldn't have to pay a stranger to do it. These are the free and official places in Franklin County that handle each step. Most are in one building downtown, at 373 S. High St.

{{ ic.icon("info") }} Before you go

  • Start with Legal Aid if someone has died and nothing was filed. It's free if you qualify.
  • Call first. Hours change, and a 2-minute call can save a trip downtown.
  • Bring your printable plan from this app. It has the home's address and parcel number.
  • Be careful of anyone who offers to buy the home or "fix the title" for a fee. These offices are free or low cost.
