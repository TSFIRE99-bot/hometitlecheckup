About Home Title Checkup

Purpose: help Central Ohio families find out whether their home is legally in the right name, and show them the free, simple steps to fix it or protect it.

Who it's for: homeowners in Franklin County, Ohio, especially older homeowners, and families who inherited a home.

How it works

  1. You type an address. The app asks the Franklin County Auditor's public parcel records (an ArcGIS REST service) who is on the deed and when the home last changed hands.
  2. You answer a few yes / no questions, like whether everyone on the deed is living.
  3. Rules based on Ohio law (Transfer on Death affidavits, survivorship deeds, and Franklin County Probate Court's small-estate options) turn the answers into a status and an action plan you can print.
  4. An optional Homestead Benefits Check shows which Franklin County property tax breaks the family may be able to claim, and how to apply. Only the County Auditor decides who qualifies.

Privacy

  • One address at a time. The app never builds lists or maps of homes that might have tangled titles, because people who prey on heirs' property could misuse them.
  • The property map shows neighbors only what the County's own public map shows (address, owner, lot size), never a title status or anything else about their family.
  • Nothing you type is saved. The server keeps no record between clicks, and the owner names shown are already public on the County Auditor's website.

Limits

  • General information, not legal advice. Every plan points to free legal help.
  • Franklin County only (for now). Owner names come from the County's records and can lag behind recent filings.
  • The app can't see whether someone has died; it asks you.

Built with

Python, Flask, requests, fpdf2, folium and Leaflet (map), and pytest, hosted on Vercel. Map tiles from OpenStreetMap contributors and Esri World Imagery. Parcel data from the Franklin County Auditor.
